# Asset provenance

All 24 modular asset variants and the assembled arena are original geometry authored in Blender for this repository on 2026-09-05. No external models, textures, HDRIs, or material libraries were downloaded.

- Creative direction: creative-director agent, recorded in ART_DIRECTION.md.
- Architectural modeling: architecture-modeler agent, reproducible in scripts/architecture.py.
- Organic modeling: nature-modeler agent, reproducible in scripts/nature.py.
- Terrain, layout assembly, lighting, export and revision: primary agent, scripts/build_scene.py.

These project-created assets introduce no third-party attribution requirements.
