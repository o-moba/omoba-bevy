# Open Moba names and branding

We use **Open Moba**, **Omoba**, and project logos to identify the official
project and its releases. The software and content licenses do not themselves
grant trademark rights. This policy does not assert that any name is registered,
that a generic term is exclusively owned, or that rights exist in every country.
Any enforceable rights remain subject to applicable law.

You may truthfully refer to the project in discussion, reviews, tutorials,
compatibility descriptions and credits. You may describe an independent game
as “based on Open Moba” or “compatible with Open Moba” when accurate. Make its
independent status clear; descriptive attribution does not require approval.

For a modified build or independently operated service, choose a distinct
product/service name and logo, identify your operator, and avoid implying
endorsement, official status or affiliation. Preserve copyright, license and
source attribution even when replacing the visible branding. Redistributors of
unmodified official builds may identify them accurately and should distinguish
the distributor from the project.

Do not use project identifiers to impersonate the official service or suggest
sponsorship that has not been granted. Requests for endorsement or logo use
beyond truthful reference can be made through the maintainers listed on the
[project repository](https://github.com/o-moba/omoba-bevy).

These rules concern origin and confusion about affiliation. They do not prohibit
commercial forks, criticism, comparison, nominative/fair use, or other uses
permitted by law, and they do not amend the standard software/content licenses.
An avatar's name, design or branding remains subject to its own rights holders'
terms. Neither a wallet connection nor an Open Moba license transfers those rights.
