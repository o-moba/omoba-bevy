# Fair play completion contract — 2026-11-05

Scope: remaining P6 / #34 / critic C5–C7. These are implementation defaults
for the owner's all-findings request, not claims of existing behavior. No live
rules or database have been changed by this document.

## Participation and AFK

- Track each human seat from actual gameplay activation, never from allocation
  or loading. Keep accounting by stable player/profile identity through socket
  changes, disconnect, AI takeover and reclaim. Bound to the round roster (32).
- Persist connected milliseconds, disconnected milliseconds, AFK takeover
  milliseconds and a sticky abandoned flag in an additive optional participation
  record. Absence of this record must preserve legacy receipt decoding/bytes.
- Ordinary transport loss has 90 seconds of reconnect grace. A continuous
  absence of at least 90 seconds marks abandon; reconnecting later cannot erase
  it. At least 90 seconds total disconnected and >=80% of the match disconnected
  also marks abandon at finalization, preventing repeated 89-second absences from evading
  accounting. Brief reconnects within the grace window are not abandons alone.
- No meaningful movement or cast/basic-attack input for 90 seconds while alive
  marks AFK and transfers the same actor to existing AI takeover. Heartbeats,
  identical idle transforms, account requests and bot actions are not activity.
  Dead/respawning time must not cause AFK; restart the grace on becoming alive.
- New meaningful authenticated input reclaims an AFK seat, without changing
  player/session/team/equipment. Only AFK detachment may auto-reclaim on input;
  an explicit Leave continues to require the existing explicit Resume path.
- Reclaim detection uses an explicit authenticated local movement-intent marker,
  latched through short taps until transmission; reflected AI poses do not
  carry that marker and cannot auto-reclaim.
- Keep `ParticipantResult.disconnected` as the actual terminal connection flag;
  do not overload it to mean a historical abandon. New award decisions use the
  participation record. Legacy disconnected receipts use a documented fallback.

## Settlement and penalties

- A ranked abandon receives no XP and a loss, including on the winning team.
  Its rating delta is at most -32 (or the normal loss if larger), clamped at zero.
- A losing non-abandon teammate of a ranked abandon receives half the normal
  loss, rounded half away from zero. Opponents and completed non-abandon winners
  keep the ordinary team Elo / placement-K award. No penalty is attributed to an
  opponent or to an unplayed allocation.
- Store abandon total plus a rolling 24-hour penalty state atomically in the
  existing exactly-once settlement transaction. Cooldowns: 5 min, 30 min, 24 h
  for first/second/third-and-later abandons in that window. Persist in PostgreSQL
  so a reconnect, lobby restart or another lobby cannot bypass it.
- Queue denial carries a stable localized code and remaining time. Refresh
  account access/penalty state asynchronously; no database call on the tick.
- Replayed outbox results, concurrent settlement, restart/adoption and a failed
  transaction cannot duplicate a strike or extend a cooldown a second time.
- A player-caused unattended shutdown (`abandoned_reaped`) still records
  proved abandons and their cooldowns; a proved ranked leaver receives -32 and
  one ranked loss. Other players receive no ordinary interrupted-match reward.
  Server crash recovery (`server_interrupted`) and duration caps are exempt,
  even if an earlier checkpoint contains an abandon flag.
- Legacy receipts with no time record suppress new positive rating/XP when
  terminally disconnected, but cannot infer a new incident or change an old
  historical winner into a loss.
- Existing pre-start dodge penalties remain distinct. P7 must remove their
  host-local bypass when cross-host queues are implemented.

## Remake and surrender

- Server-authorized, signed requests bind epoch, match, vote generation and
  authenticated roster identity. Only actual connected human teammates vote;
  bots, retained AI endpoints and strangers never do.
- Remake: before elapsed 180 seconds, a teammate absent continuously for at
  least 90 seconds permits a team vote. A strict majority of remaining connected
  human teammates passes it. No opponent can force a remake for another team.
- Remake settles with a distinct outcome, no ordinary rating/XP/win/loss or
  played totals for innocent players. A proved ranked abandon still receives
  its individual -32-or-larger loss, one played/rated loss, one strike and its
  cooldown; it receives no XP. This prevents a premade from using an early
  deliberate absence to erase a losing start. In Casual, the qualifying abandon
  records its strike/cooldown without competitive counters. Result/history
  separates the neutral remake from the leaver's personal penalty.
- Surrender: available from elapsed 600 seconds; four of five or all remaining
  connected human teammates must agree. Finish as an ordinary Completed loss
  with the other team as winner, applying the same abandon award policy.
- Votes expire after 30 seconds; a failed vote has a 120-second team cooldown.
  A remake vote's deadline is shortened to the remaining 180-second window.
  A replay, expired vote or stale generation cannot resolve the match twice.
- Client pause menu shows eligibility, timing, initiator, yes/needed and explicit
  yes/no controls; local UI never decides Victory. Localize RU/EN/zh-Hans.
