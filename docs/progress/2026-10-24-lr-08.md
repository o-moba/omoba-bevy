# LR-08: player-chosen Ranked, Casual PvP and vs Bots queues

Goal: implement [LR-08](../plans/launch-readiness.md) with the section 7 owner
defaults O-1 (Ranked at launch, humans only, a Casual offer after 5 minutes),
O-2 (Casual PvP groups every cohort, bots after 30 s, vs Bots separate) and O-3
(no Ranked entry gate). Branch `feat/lr-08` (omoba-bevy). Resumed from an earlier
attempt that left the shared wire types uncommitted; they were kept after review
(one test compared against `serde_json::Value`, which reorders keys, and was
rewritten against the exact bytes a protocol 11 client signs).

## Before

- Home and the party lobby offered only Quick and vs Bots; the Wait for players
  button was gone and its key orphaned.
- Head count decided rating: ten humans meant `release` rules and `rated`, so a
  ten-human Quick game was rated and nobody could choose an unrated PvP game.
- Quick split the few online humans by newcomer cohort and a literal 300-point
  window, into separate bot games.
- The lobby checked other units against the anchor's lowest rating but never the
  anchor party itself: a party {1000 newcomer, 1400 veteran} plus eight newcomers
  formed a rated roster that the store rejected ("Queued ratings changed…").

## Design decisions

- **Wire compatibility without a protocol bump.** `MatchPreference::{Casual,
  Ranked, BotPractice}` keep the wire names `quick`, `humans_only`,
  `bot_practice`. An old Quick search plays Casual PvP. Everything new is
  additive and skipped when empty: `find_match.also_casual` (the signed bytes of
  a request without it are unchanged), `Waiting.{searching,
  casual_offer_after_secs, also_casual}`, and the failure code
  `ranked_party_cohort`. Protocol 12 stays reserved for LR-67.
- **The queue decides the rules.** `Manifest::match_mode`: only Ranked runs
  `release` (rated) rules. Casual PvP runs worker `practice` rules like vs Bots:
  bots take the empty seats, none when ten humans queued, and the round settles
  as `public-casual-v1` with the new reason `casual_queue` (vs Bots keeps
  `allocated_bots`). The store now accepts a `casual_queue` roster without bots.
  `rating_eligibility` also refuses any non-Ranked worker.
- **One cohort policy.** `career-store/src/matchmaking.rs` exports `fits_cohort`
  and `same_cohort`; the career queue, the lobby's Ranked selection and the
  store's rated-start check all use them. `server/src/match_service.rs` has no
  rating literal.
- **Selection order.** vs Bots first, then Ranked, then Casual PvP. A Ranked entry
  that took the Casual offer keeps its place: a full Ranked roster still takes it
  first. Its Casual bot fill counts from the moment it took the offer, not from
  its Ranked queue time. A party searches Casual only once every member took the
  offer (units must share one queue).
- **A mixed Ranked party is told, not left waiting.** Its members get
  `Failed{ranked_party_cohort}`, shown as "Your party's ratings are too far apart
  for Ranked. Cancel and play Casual PvP together."
- **Market-neutral.** Nothing here depends on the market or the region; no new
  market comparisons, no payment links.

## Changes

- `shared/src/match_service.rs`: queue variants with the old wire names,
  `MatchPreference::rated`, additive `Waiting` fields; `shared/src/career.rs`:
  `FindMatch.also_casual`; `wire_enums.rs` variant list.
- `career-store`: `fits_cohort`, `same_cohort` (queue and store use them);
  `validate_public_casual` accepts `casual_queue` with 0–9 bots and a recovered
  interruption of any roster.
- `server`:
  - `match_service.rs`: `Search`, `Waiting.casual_since`, `searches`,
    `select` returning the queue, `waiting_view` (queue count, Casual offer,
    mixed-party failure), `rated` from the queue; constants `CASUAL_BOT_FILL`
    (30 s) and `RANKED_CASUAL_OFFER` (300 s).
  - `match_allocation.rs` `Manifest::match_mode`, used by `match_pool.rs` and
    `runtime/mod.rs`; `career_runtime.rs` eligibility and unrated reason;
    `career_port.rs` `MatchRequest`.
- `client`:
  - `match_service.rs`: `also_casual` on the search, `CasualOffer`,
    `take_casual_offer`, `badge`/`match_badge`, `queue_name`/`queue_badge`, the
    queue count in the status, localized failure codes (`queue.failed.<code>`).
  - Home: **CASUAL PVP** as the main button, **Ranked** and **vs Bots** side by
    side under it (one row, so the phone column keeps its height), Offline
    practice unchanged; a party leader launches the party into the chosen queue.
  - Party lobby: PLAY VS BOTS plus **Casual PvP** and **Ranked** on a public
    lobby.
  - Searching: queue badge, queue count, the Casual offer line and button.
    Draft: badge next to Cancel. Loading: badge first on the status row.
  - New `queue` namespace in en, ru, zh-Hans; `career.unrated.casual_queue`;
    lobby hints no longer promise bots in Ranked. Removed keys:
    `home.button.humans_only` (orphan), `home.play.quick_match`,
    `home.button.bot_practice`, `lobby.button.quick_match`. zh-Hans uses 匹配对战
    for Casual PvP (休闲 is outside the display font subset).
- Docs: `match-progression.md` (queues table, low-population Ranked policy),
  `public-mvp.md`, `features.md` (new section and the stale "Wait for players"
  line), `party.md`, `bot-practice-and-social.md`, architecture `system.md`,
  `protocols.md`, `decisions.md` (ADR-015 note), launch-readiness acceptance.
