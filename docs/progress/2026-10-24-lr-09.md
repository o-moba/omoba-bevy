# LR-09: arena capacity, code parts

Goal: everything LR-09 needs before the RU host exists. A full arena pool tells the
player where they stand and offers Offline practice in one tap; both connection
ceilings answer "server full" instead of dropping; the lobby reports a busy pool and
the RU host alerts on it; the load probe is ready to measure the host and size the
capacity ([plan](../plans/launch-readiness.md#lr-09--arena-capacity-measured-and-sized-on-the-ru-host)).
The measurement and the final capacity, port range and cgroup limits need the host.

## Changes

- **Line for a free arena** (`server/src/match_service.rs`, `Lobby::refresh_line`).
  While every arena is busy the lobby replays its own selection (`select`: vs Bots,
  then Ranked, then Casual PvP) over the queue each pass and gives every ready roster
  its 1-based place; a roster the per-source limits (LR-16) would refuse takes none.
  A newcomer to a full lobby gets its place at once. `capacity_wait` is now set only
  for such ready rosters: a Ranked search at 3/10 keeps showing its own progress
  instead of «All arenas are busy».
- **Estimate** (`server/src/capacity.rs`, `Turnover`): the pool's occupied count is
  compared before and after each pass, so arenas that freed are counted;
  `eta_secs = position × window / frees` over the last 15 minutes (none before a minute
  of uptime or the first free).
- **Wire** (`shared/src/match_service.rs`): `Waiting.capacity_line{position, eta_secs?}`,
  additive like LR-08's fields (no protocol bump; an older client shows its plain
  capacity copy).
- **Searching screen** (`client/src/match_service.rs`, `client/src/frontend/searching.rs`):
  «All arenas are busy · you are number 2 in line · about 3 min» and the button
  **Play offline practice while you wait** with the line «Offline practice leaves the
  queue. Search again when you finish.» One tap sends `StartOffline` (the lobby drops
  the entry through the signed Leave) and opens the practice picker with the chosen
  hero, as Home's Offline practice does. The offer also shows while the server is too
  full to sign in. Copy in en, ru and zh-Hans (`client/i18n/*/capacity.json`).
- **Endpoint ceiling** (`server/src/public_transport.rs`): at 512 endpoint records a
  new source's probe is answered `PublicServerDatagram::ServerFull{client_nonce}`
  (echoes its nonce, never larger than the probe, inside the probe budget; the per-IP
  limit still drops). The variant is additive without a protocol bump: it replaces
  silence, and an older client drops a datagram it cannot decode
  (`shared/src/protocol/wire_enums.rs` records why). The client
  (`client/src/net/public_transport.rs`, `transport.rs`, `session.rs`) checks the
  nonce, keeps probing and shows «The server is full. Retrying…» on Home, the search
  screen and the connection panel for 10 s after the last answer.
- **Career-client ceiling** (`server/src/career_backend.rs`): at 512 clients a new
  endpoint's view carries `error_code` `server_full` (stateless: unknown endpoint and
  a full table). The client shows `admission.error.server_full` and keeps signing in at
  its 2 s pace instead of timing out after 25 s (`client/src/career_identity.rs`), so it
  gets in once a place frees.
- **Capacity-busy metric** (`server/src/capacity.rs`, `CapacityMetric`): the lobby
  rewrites `<match root>/capacity.json` (capacity, occupied, searching,
  waiting_for_arena, busy_since_ms, longest_wait_secs, busy_periods) every 5 s and at
  once when the line forms or clears, and logs `CAPACITY_METRIC event=busy|still_busy|clear`
  at those moments and every minute while busy.
- **Alert** (`ops/vps/ru/ru_monitor.py`, LR-17's host check): "players have waited for a
  free arena for N min" once `busy_since_ms` is `OMOBA_ALERT_CAPACITY_WAIT_SECONDS` (120,
  `alerts.env.example`) old in a fresh report while the lobby runs; a missing (older
  lobby) or stale report is no alert. Resolves like every other alert.
- **Load probe** (`scripts/test_public_mvp.mjs`), ready for the host: it runs from the
  file alone (`--contract=compatibility.json` or `--protocol-version`), `--mix` of queues,
  `--capacity`, `--max-wait-secs`, `--limits-from=<unit>` (Linux: `systemd-run --scope`
  with the unit's MemoryHigh/MemoryMax/CPUQuota/TasksMax, so the lobby and its workers
  share them as on the host), per-arena-type report (snapshot gap p50/p95/p99, time to
  Running, snapshot bytes/s per player), lobby + workers CPU from cumulative CPU time
  (`/proc` on Linux) and RSS while everyone plays, `CAPACITY_RESULT` summary lines, and
  `--scenario=capacity-wait`. It now lifts the per-source limits (LR-16) for its own
  lobby: all its players come from 127.0.0.1, and since LR-16 a multi-client run was
  refused after the first arena.
- **Pool** (`server/src/match_pool.rs`): `occupied()`, `capacity()`, `root()`.
- Docs: [ops/vps/ru/README.md › Arena capacity](../../ops/vps/ru/README.md#arena-capacity-lr-09)
  (current values, the per-arena table to fill, the full-pool behaviour, the
  measurement procedure), the monitoring table and knobs there, `alerts.env.example`,
  `docs/public-mvp.md`, `docs/architecture/{protocols,system,data,identity-and-accounts}.md`,
  `docs/features.md`, the plan's LR-09 acceptance and LR-17's capacity-wait alert line.

None of it is market-specific: no new market branches, host names or literals.

- **Owner (O-16):** if 20 vs Bots arenas do not fit the 2 vCPU / 4 GB host shared with
  PostgreSQL, the Account API and the portal, more quota or a bigger host.

  process; the RU host never gets near it at launch sizes. The per-IP limits (LR-16)
  still drop silently by design.
- LR-28 takes the measured snapshot bytes per player as its input; LR-10's "more arenas
  per vCPU" is read from the same probe on the host.
