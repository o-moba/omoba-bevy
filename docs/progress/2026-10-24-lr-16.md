# LR-16: admission and abuse limits

Goal: one source (an address, or a household or carrier NAT behind one) can no
longer lock everyone else out of login, create profiles without bound, or hold the
arenas; profiles that never played do not pile up; the RU host drops per-source
UDP floods in the kernel ([plan](../plans/launch-readiness.md#lr-16--admission-and-abuse-limits)).

## Changes

- **Per-source budgets** (`server/src/admission.rs`, `server/src/public_transport.rs`).
  Transport probes and compatibility replies each have a one-second budget: 8 per IP,
  32 per network (IPv4 /24; IPv6 /64 is one IP, /48 one network), then 64 in all, of
  which the last 16 only a source's first datagram of the second may take. Only
  admitted datagrams are recorded, so the tables stay within the global budget.
  One IP holds at most 8 endpoints.
- **Career clients** (`server/src/career_backend.rs`): at most 8 per IP; at the
  limit the IP's stalest client idle for 30 s (not in a match) gives way.
- **New profiles** (career schema 4, `career-store/migrations/postgres/004_admission_limits.sql`,
  `CareerStore::authenticate_from`): a new key creates a profile only while its IP
  created fewer than 10 and everyone fewer than 5000 in the last 24 hours. The
  counter is durable (`career_profile_admissions`, a salted SHA-256 of the IP,
  rows deleted after 48 h) and serialized by one transaction lock. A refusal carries
  `CareerView.error_code` (`new_profile_limit`, `new_profiles_paused`; additive, no
  protocol bump); the client shows it in en, ru and zh-Hans
  (`client/i18n/*/admission.json`) on the career panel and the searching screen and
  stops re-sending that sign-in.
- **Arenas** (`server/src/match_service.rs`): the lobby remembers each FindMatch's
  IP in memory. A roster whose every human comes from one IP that already holds one
  live arena alone gets `arena_limit` (vs Bots at once, a bot-filled Casual roster
  when its fill is due). Three vs Bots allocations from an IP that ended before they
  ran, within 10 minutes, give a 120 s `practice_cooldown`. Copy in
  `client/i18n/*/queue.json`.
- **Retention**: `career_prune_unplayed_profiles` (SECURITY DEFINER, `search_path`
  pinned to the migration's schemas plus `pg_temp`, no PUBLIC execute) deletes
  profiles whose only key is older than 30 days, with no match, XP, friend, other or
  revoked key or live presence; any other referencing row (portal sessions, settings,
  sign-in links, Supporter grants) keeps the profile through its foreign key. The
  lobby's career worker runs it hourly, 200 at a time, from 5 minutes after start,
  and logs `PROFILE_RETENTION pruned=N`. The game role gets EXECUTE but still cannot
  delete a key (`account-api/ops/grants.sql`).
- **Reconnect storms**: one Login per endpoint and challenge in flight (10 s), a full
  worker queue is no longer an error ("Account service is busy" is gone for sign-in;
  the client retries), and sign-ins or account actions that arrive before the
  database is connected wait up to 10 s instead of failing at once.
- **Settings**: every limit is an `OMOBA_LIMIT_*` variable (plus
  `OMOBA_PROFILE_RETENTION_DAYS`), read once at start; a bad value stops the server;
  the lobby logs `Admission limits: …`. None is market-specific.
- **RU host kit**: `ops/vps/ru/nftables-omoba.conf` (own table `inet omoba_limits`,
  per-source rates: lobby 400/s burst 800, arenas 1200/s burst 2400) and
  `omoba-limits-ru.service` (oneshot). `ru_host.py` checks the file against the lobby
  unit's ports and refuses `flush ruleset` or other tables, installs it after
  `nft -c`, enables and starts the unit, and `status` reports a missing or unloaded
  table. The `nftables` package joins the bootstrap packages; the documented knobs in
  `lobby.env` no longer warn. Knobs: [ops/vps/ru/README.md › Admission limits](../../ops/vps/ru/README.md#admission-limits-lr-16).
- **Harness**: `ServerProcess::restart` (same address and environment) and
  `harness/tests/admission.rs`.
- Docs: `docs/architecture/{data,protocols,system,decisions,identity-and-accounts}.md`,
  `docs/public-mvp.md`, `docs/release-compatibility.md`, `docs/features.md`,
  `career-store/migrations/postgres/README.md`, `account-api/README.md`, the plan's
  LR-16 acceptance.
