# 2026-10-25 — One app per platform and iOS tier auras: spec LR-68, LR-69, ADR-025

## Goal

The owner decided on 2026-10-24:
- one production app per platform for Russia and the international market, where the account's region decides servers, sign-in and payments;
- website donations stay;
- the iPhone app sells the auras through Apple in-app purchases, chosen and paid in the profile card, with fixed prices from about $1 to $100 and no link to web payments;
- purchases may simply not work in Russia.

The owner left the price ladder to us. This session turned those decisions into specs before any code.

## Method

A workflow ran:
- three independent research passes: the IAP product and current StoreKit code; region architecture against `origin/dev`; store and legal compliance from primary sources;
- one synthesis;
- three adversarial reviews: compliance (15 problems, 1 blocker), engineering (25 problems, 1 blocker) and product/UX (16 problems);
- a revision that verified each point.

Every file and line citation in the spec was re-read at omoba-bevy `e4bdc6c` and omoba-web `8fe4d68`.

### Conflicts resolved during synthesis

Baseline: omoba-bevy `origin/dev` @ `e4bdc6c` (2026-10-25), omoba-web `origin/dev` @ `8fe4d68`. Every path cited below was re-read at these commits.

1. **RU names and zones are already done.** PR #121 (`a32cacc`) moved RU to `lobby.omoba.ru:4000`, `https://account.omoba.ru` and `https://omoba.ru` (`scripts/markets.py` `MARKETS["ru"]`). `client/src/session_config.rs:41` already reads `["omoba.io", "ekza.io", "omoba.ru"]`. R0 does not repeat that work.
2. **Jade costs $2.49.** That keeps the 1 : 2.5 : 5 : 10 : 25 : 50 : 100 ratio of the ruble tiers, and it is a valid Apple price point.
3. **Points are whole rubles.** YooKassa gives `amount_minor / 100` rounded down, recomputed after refunds. Each Apple product carries the points of its tier threshold.
4. **Storefront prices are left to Apple.** Apple equalizes them automatically from the US base. Points come from the product, not the price. Manual CIS prices are an option in O-29. Products go live only after O-24.
5. **No production App Store notification URL until R2b and counsel.** One notification URL per app would send RU-account payloads to the international host. First purchases are found through device delivery. Refunds come from a daily poll of refund history. Each region stores only its own accounts' Apple transactions.
6. **No live monthly Apple subscribers exist.** Global production runs no Account API (`docs/architecture/system.md:181,198,201`). `billing/` has no HTTP verifier. `docs/supporter.md:14-20` blocks Apple checkout. So the monthly product is retired with no migration.
7. **One app identity per platform (O-21).** The compile-time `ru` market stays for RU server binaries, CI and the M0 RU packages until R3.
8. **iOS RU sign-in label.** The neutral label is dropped. All channels name VK ID, OK and Mail.ru plainly. The 4.8 answer is the first-party equivalent options on the same sheet, not the label.
9. **Sign in with Apple and 149-FZ.** It is international-only, has no scopes, and is gated by Russian signals. It moves to R2b, behind O-22, O-23 and O-31.
10. **Google Play.** Play has waived its billing requirement for payments from users in Russia since 2022-08-02 (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11950272). So `(ru, google_play)` links to `omoba.ru/supporter`, and `(global, google_play)` has no purchase entry.
11. **Account deletion.** The in-app deletion request is required on every store channel: Apple 5.1.1(v), plus Play's in-app and web deletion rule. Each region's portal also gets a public `/delete` page.
12. **Host location.** The repository does not record where the global host (`77.246.105.57`) is. The spec makes no claim about it (O-30, O-31).
13. **Phasing.** R2 splits in two:
    - R2a: RU on the unified iOS app, needing only `account.omoba.ru` plus an international deletion intake.
    - R2b: the international Account API, Sign in with Apple and international IAP.

### Review points rejected

- **Review 2 (summary): "using Transaction.all for restore is right".** Rejected. Apple's `Transaction.all` page lists unfinished consumables, non-consumables and auto-renewable subscriptions, not non-renewing subscriptions (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/transaction/all). Restore moves to server-side transaction history, as review 1 proposed.
- **Review 2 (accept_apple): "Restore submits only transactions whose token appears in the profile's token list returned by apple_prepare".** Superseded. Restore is now server-side and routes each transaction by its own token. Pending foreign transactions are held back on the device by the device-root `{token: region}` map.
- **Review 2 (VK strings): channel variants for every `devices.vk.*` and `devices.button.vk_link` key.** Superseded. The neutral `app_store` label is dropped (review 1 and review 3, 4.8), so VK, OK and Mail.ru are named plainly on all channels and the strings need no variants.
- **Review 3 (Switching): rebind the region inside the process on phones.** Rejected in favour of review 3's own alternative, the blocking restart screen. Rebinding would have to tear down process-wide statics (connection profile, passport store, identity, `ekza://` root) and running sessions. A first bind on first run stays in-process because nothing region-scoped exists before it.
- **Review 3 (stacking outcome): a server preview endpoint `GET /v1/me/supporter/preview`.** Rejected. Per-grant `tier_points` and `valid_until` in `SupporterStatus` (protocol 12) let the client compute the outcome line with the shared `for_points`, with no new route. The rest of that point (outcome line, cheapest product that reaches a tier, Extend only on the top tier) is applied.
- **Review 3 (CIS prices): "Apple's equalized prices there are often below the web ruble tiers", so manual CIS prices should be the default.** Partly rejected. The review gives no source for systematic underpricing, and equalization targets parity with the US base. Manual CIS pricing is an option in O-29, and the default stays automatic. Making O-24 a gate before products go live is applied.
- **Review 2 (Ekza SDK): add client or resolver injection to the SDK.** Not adopted. The SDK is an external crate pinned to `d18ec72`. The spec takes the review's other option: no SDK client is constructed in RU, which the network-audit boot proves.
