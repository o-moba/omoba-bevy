# LR-15: moderation minimum (sanctions, chat kill switch, operator CLI)

Goal: an operator can mute, ban from matchmaking or Ranked, suspend or force-rename a
profile, lift any of it without a restart, and switch chat off server-wide; every action
is audited and kept at least a year; RU servers launch with chat off (owner default O-9)
([plan](../plans/launch-readiness.md#lr-15--moderation-minimum-sanctions-chat-kill-switch-operator-cli)).

## Changes

- **Career schema 5** (`career-store/migrations/postgres/005_sanctions.sql`, the number
  reserved in section 7): `career_sanctions` (profile, kind `chat_mute` /
  `matchmaking_ban` / `ranked_ban` / `account_ban` / `name_reset`, reason, created at and
  by, `expires_at` or until lifted, lifted at, by and why), `career_moderation_events`
  (append-only audit, no foreign key; a trigger refuses UPDATE and deleting a row younger
  than a year) and `career_chat_switch` (the database-wide switch). Store API in
  `career-store/src/moderation.rs`: `access` (key, current name and active sanctions in
  one query), `active_sanctions`, `chat_enabled`, and for operators `add_sanction`,
  `lift_sanction`, `sanctions`, `profile_keys`, `find_profile`, `moderation_events`,
  `set_chat`. A `name_reset` renames to `Player#NNNN` (a free tag) in the same
  transaction; `rename` refuses while it is active. A sanction's foreign key keeps its
  profile out of the LR-16 unplayed-profile retention, so a ban on a throwaway key holds.
- **Shared contract** (`shared/src/moderation.rs`): `SanctionKind` (tolerant: an unknown
  kind decodes to `Unknown` and restricts nothing; classified in `wire_enums`),
  `Sanction {kind, until?}`, refusal codes, `queue_refusal`, `hides_presence`,
  `OMOBA_CHAT` parsing. Additive wire fields, no protocol bump: `CareerView.sanctions`,
  `error_code` `account_banned` / `name_locked`, `SocialView.{error_code, chat_disabled,
  mute}`, lobby `Failed{matchmaking_ban|ranked_ban}`.
- **Game server.** Sign-in reads the profile's sanctions and refuses an account ban
  (`LoginRefusal` carries the ban to the client); the 10-second access refresh now runs
  `CareerStore::access` and applies sanctions and a reset name, and ends a session whose
  account was banned meanwhile. `FindMatch` is refused by a matchmaking ban (any queue) or
  a ranked ban (Ranked) and leaves the queue (`MatchService::refuse`); the standalone rated
  queue refuses too. Chat: a muted player's messages are refused before a rate token is
  spent and their held messages are dropped; with chat off (`OMOBA_CHAT=off`, read once at
  start and inherited by workers, or the database switch the career worker polls every
  5 s) every chat message is refused and held chat dropped; reactions stay. Party
  presence withdraws a player with an active account or matchmaking ban (out of
  presence, party and invites); the next presence carries a reset name.
- **Account API.** `refuse_banned`: every session-authenticated route, pairing approval
  and completion and VK sign-in answer `403 account_banned` (sessions are not revoked, so
  they work again after the ban); `PATCH /v1/me/profile` answers `403 name_locked`
  during a name reset. Operator commands in the same binary
  (`account-api/src/moderation.rs`, `main.rs`): `player`, `sanction add|list|lift`,
  `rename`, `chat [status|on|off]`, with required `--reason` and `--by` and an explicit
  `--for <30m|12h|7d|2w>` or `--permanent`. Grants: the game role reads sanctions and the
  switch; the portal role may apply and lift (only the lift columns), append audit rows
  and switch chat; no runtime role may delete either.
- **Client.** `client/src/moderation.rs` and `client/i18n/{en,ru,zh-Hans}/moderation.json`:
  every refusal in the player's language with its end in UTC. The search screen and the
  career panel show the suspended sign-in and the search bans; the match chat shows
  «muted until …» or «chat is turned off», refuses to send and opens without a keyboard.
  A suspended client stops re-sending its sign-in and starts over every 60 s
  (`SUSPENDED_RETRY`), so a lifted ban needs no restart; an account ban during a session
  restarts the handshake like a revoked device. No market comparison was added: chat off
  is a per-deployment server setting.
- **RU host kit.** `ops/vps/ru/omoba-lobby-ru.service` sets `OMOBA_CHAT=off`;
  `ru_host.py` refuses a lobby unit without it (`UNIT_ENV_RULES`, test in
  `scripts/test_ru_host.py`).
- Docs: `RUNBOOK.md` › Moderation (look up, apply, rename, lift, chat, the record, limits;
  the `systemd-run` invocation on the RU host), `docs/features.md`,
  `docs/architecture/{data,protocols,identity-and-accounts,system}.md`,
  `career-store/migrations/postgres/README.md`, `account-api/README.md`,
  `account-api/docs/openapi.json`, `ops/vps/ru/README.md`, the plan's LR-15 acceptance.
